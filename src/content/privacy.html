Privacy Policy

What Gro collects, who can see it, who else touches it, and how to get rid of it.

Last updated September 8, 2026

The short version

Gro is a small app run by two people, Camden Brown and Kasen Afflerbach. We collect what the app needs to work and nothing else.

There is no advertising, no analytics, no tracking, and no third-party code in the app that watches what you do. We do not sell your information and we never will. We do not know where you are.

You can delete your account, and everything attached to it, from inside the app at any time.

Who we are

Gro is operated by Camden Brown and Kasen Afflerbach. You can reach us at gro.foundation26@gmail.com, and the same address appears on the You tab inside the app.

We are the people who decide what happens to your information, and we are also the only two people who can see the parts of it that are not public.

What we collect, and why

When you create an account:

  • Your email address — so we can send you a sign-in code, let you reset your password, and reach you about your account.
  • A password — stored only as a cryptographic hash by our authentication provider. We never see it and we cannot recover it.
  • Your name, as you type it. This is shown publicly on your profile, your comments and your posts, so use whatever you are happy for other people to see.

When you answer the two onboarding questions:

  • What you are chasing, and the free-text version if none of the options fit.
  • Where you are at right now.
  • Your device's time zone, so the app can work out when your day starts.

If you choose to add them:

  • A short bio.
  • A profile photo.

As you use the app:

  • Comments you write.
  • Posts you like and people you follow.
  • Reports you file and people you block.
  • For the two accounts allowed to post: the posts themselves — the photo or video, a still taken from the first frame of a video, the caption, the tags, and the date the card runs.

Created automatically:

  • An account identifier, so your rows can be told apart from everyone else’s.
  • Timestamps for when things were created.
  • A flag recording whether an account is one of the two administrator accounts.

What we do not collect:

  • Your location, in any form. The app never asks for it.
  • Your contacts, calendar, microphone or camera roll beyond the single photo or video you choose to attach.
  • Advertising identifiers. There is no advertising in Gro.
  • Analytics or behavioural data. There is no analytics SDK in the app, so nobody — including us — has a record of which cards you looked at or how long for.
  • Payment information. Gro is free and takes no payments.

Who can see what

Anyone signed in to Gro can see your name, your profile photo, your bio, your comments and — if you are one of the two accounts that post — your posts. Treat all of that as public.

Your email address is never shown to other users. Neither is your password, to anyone, including us. Your onboarding answers are not shown on your profile.

Nothing in Gro is visible without an account. There is no public web page showing your profile or your posts.

Who else touches your information

We use a small number of companies to run the app. Each one only receives what it needs for its part, may not use it for anything of its own, and is required by its agreement with us to protect it to at least the standard described in this policy.

  • Supabase — hosts the database, the authentication system and the uploaded files. It holds everything listed above. Data is stored in the United States, in Amazon Web Services’ Oregon region.
  • Resend — sends the account emails. It receives your email address and the contents of that email, such as a verification code.
  • Expo — delivers the app and its updates to your phone. It does not receive your account information.
  • Apple and Google — distribute the app through their stores. What they collect as the store is covered by their own policies, not this one.

That is the complete list. We do not share your information with advertisers, data brokers, or anyone else, and we do not sell it.

Tracking

We do not track you. Nothing in Gro links what you do here with information about you from anywhere else, and nothing is shared with a data broker or an advertising network.

That is why the app never asks for permission to track you: there is nothing to ask about.

How long we keep it, and how to delete it

We keep your information for as long as your account exists. There is no separate archive and no analytics copy sitting somewhere else.

You can delete your account from inside the app: open the You tab and choose Delete my account. It asks you to type a word to confirm, because there is no undo.

Deleting your account removes:

  • The account itself, and your ability to sign in.
  • Your profile — name, bio, photo, time zone and onboarding answers.
  • Every comment you have written.
  • Your likes, the people you follow, the people you have blocked, and the reports you filed.
  • For the two accounts that post: every post, and its place in the feed.
  • The files you uploaded.

We retain nothing afterwards. If an uploaded file cannot be removed for a technical reason we still delete the account, and the file is then unreachable — there is no account, profile or post pointing at it.

You can also delete individual comments and posts without deleting your account, and you can change your name, bio and photo at any time under Edit profile. Withdrawing your consent to any of this means deleting the content, or the account.

Your choices

  • See and change what we hold: the You tab, then Edit profile.
  • Delete a single comment or post: the Delete option on it.
  • Delete everything: the You tab, then Delete my account.
  • Ask for a copy of what we hold about you: email gro.foundation26@gmail.com from the address on the account and we will send it.
  • Stop hearing from someone: block them. It works in both directions.

Depending on where you live you may have further rights over your information — to see it, correct it, delete it, or object to how it is used. Email us and we will honour them; we will not ask you why.

Young people

Gro is for people aged 13 and over. If you live somewhere that sets a higher minimum age for using a service like this without a parent's permission, that age applies to you instead.

We do not knowingly collect anything from anyone under 13. If we find out that an account belongs to someone younger, we delete it and everything attached to it. If you believe a child has an account here, email gro.foundation26@gmail.com and we will deal with it.

How we protect it

  • Everything travels between your phone and our servers over an encrypted connection.
  • Passwords are stored as hashes, never as text, and cannot be read by us.
  • The database refuses reads and writes it has not been explicitly told to allow, row by row, rather than trusting the app to ask nicely.
  • Only the two administrator accounts can see reports, and nobody at all can read another person’s email address through the app.

No system is perfect and we will not pretend otherwise. If something goes wrong that affects your information, we will tell you.

Changes to this policy
